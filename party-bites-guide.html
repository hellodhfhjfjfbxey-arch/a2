Guide 02

Party Bites Planning

How many bites, how many kinds and how far ahead? A simple plan keeps the host relaxed and the trays full.

Serves
8 to 40
Steps
5
Read
4 min
1

How many bites per guest

  • Before dinner: 3 to 4 bites per guest.
  • A two-hour drinks party: 8 to 10 bites per guest.
  • Instead of dinner: 12 to 15 bites per guest.
2

How many kinds

For up to 12 guests, four or five kinds is plenty. For larger parties, six to eight. Balance them: something meaty, something vegetarian, something crunchy, something creamy and one small sweet bite to finish.

3

Make-ahead plan

Bake bases and make dips a day ahead and store them separately. Chop toppings in the morning. Assemble crisp bites in the last hour, and keep warm bites in a low oven until the doorbell rings.

4

Serving and flow

Send trays out in waves rather than all at once, so food stays fresh and guests have something to look forward to. Put napkins and small bowls for skewers near every tray.

5

Dietary needs

Ask guests ahead about allergies and preferences. Label bites with small cards and keep allergen-free food on a separate tray with its own serving tools.
