About Morsels

We think the best part of the party fits in one hand.

Morsels is an independent home-cooking website about bite-sized food: canapés, crostini and party bites.

We share ideas, techniques and plans written for home cooks, with free tools that help you work out quantities and timings. Every guide is cooked at home before it is published.

Independent

We do not sell food or kitchenware, use affiliate links or accept payment for mentions.

Private

Our calculator and timeline run in your browser and store nothing about you.

Checked

Every guide is reviewed by a second editor, dated and corrected when readers write in.
