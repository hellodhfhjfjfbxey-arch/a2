About the kitchen

We think the best part of the party fits in one hand.

Morsels is an independent home-cooking website about bite-sized food.

We share ideas, techniques and plans for canapés, crostini and party bites, written for home cooks. Our bite builder and pairing grid make it easy to invent your own.

We do not sell food or kitchenware, use affiliate links or accept payment for mentions. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
