Bite-Size Basics

A great bite is a whole dish in miniature. Get the base, spread, topping and finish right and everything else follows.

Serves
Any number
Courses
5
Read
4 min
1

The one-bite rule

Party food should be eaten in one or two neat bites, without cutlery and without dripping. Aim for pieces about 4 to 5 cm across. If guests need a napkin and a plate, it is a starter, not a bite.

2

Choose a sturdy base

  • Toasted crostini or crackers for crunch.
  • Cucumber or chicory leaves for freshness.
  • Puff pastry or tartlet cases for richness.
  • Blinis or mini pancakes for softness.
3

Spread, then top

A spread such as cream cheese, hummus or butter acts as glue and protects the base from soggy toppings. Then add one bold topping: smoked fish, roasted vegetables, cured meat or a little cheese.

4

Finish with something fresh

A herb leaf, a squeeze of lemon, a few flakes of salt or a drizzle of oil makes a bite look and taste finished. Contrast is the goal: crisp with creamy, rich with sharp.

5

Assemble at the last moment

Prepare every element ahead, but assemble wet toppings onto crisp bases as close to serving as possible. Bites made more than an hour ahead lose their crunch.
